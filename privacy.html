Trivia Rescue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026. Last updated: 13 October 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how Udobiz Associated LTD ("Udobiz", "we",
"us") collects, uses, shares and protects information in connection with the
Trivia Rescue mobile game (the "App", package com.Kosy.TriviaRescue).
By downloading or using the App, you agree to this Policy.

Data controller: Udobiz Associated LTD.
Contact: kosynom@gmail.com.

1. Summary

Trivia Rescue is a game with no account. We do not ask for your name, email
address or phone number, and nothing you type into the game is sent to us.
We collect a limited set of data to show ads, to understand how the game is
played and to deliver in-app purchases, as described below. We do not sell
your personal information.

2. Information we collect

The App collects the following automatically, through the third-party SDKs
it uses:

Data Source Why
Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA) Google AdMob Show ads and measure ad performance
Approximate location (derived from your IP address, never GPS) Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics Ad delivery and coarse regional analytics
App activity (for example: a level started or finished, a question answered, an ad shown, a setting changed) Firebase Analytics Understand and improve the game
Purchase history (which in-app product was bought) Apple App Store or Google Play billing, Firebase Analytics Deliver purchases and prevent duplicate credits

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you email us), phone
number, contacts, precise location, photos, messages or health data.

What stays on your device. Your progress, settings, the age you may
enter in Settings and the display name you may choose are stored only on
your device. The display name travels only inside a challenge link you
choose to share yourself, and only to the people you send it to. The
optional "come back" reminder is a local notification scheduled by the App
on your device; it involves no server.

3. How we use information and our legal bases

We use the information above to operate, maintain and improve the App, to
serve and measure advertising, to process in-app purchases, and to detect
and fix problems.

For users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland,
our legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR are:

  • Consent, for personalised advertising and the associated advertising
    identifier and analytics, obtained through the in-app consent form
    described in Section 5. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests, for non-personalised advertising, non-personalised
    analytics and protecting the App from abuse.
  • Performance of a contract, for processing your in-app purchases.

4. How information is shared

We share information only as follows:

  • Google. The App uses Google's SDKs, which process data on our behalf
    and, for advertising, as independent parties: Google AdMob (advertising;
    the advertising identifier and approximate location may be shared with
    Google and its advertising partners to serve and measure ads) and Firebase
    Analytics (usage analytics). Google's handling of this data is governed by
    Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and, for
    advertising, https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Apple and Google, for purchases. In-app purchases are processed by the
    App Store or Google Play under their own terms; we receive a record of the
    purchase, never your payment details.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or to
    protect rights, safety or the integrity of the App.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for
purposes other than those described here.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the App shows a
    Google-certified consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) so you can
    accept or decline personalised ads. Change your choice at any time through
    Settings > Privacy options in the App.
  • iOS: Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether the App may
    track you. You can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security >
    Tracking    .
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad
    personalisation, in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Age: if you tell the App in Settings > Age for ads that you are
    under 13, or do not enter an age at all, the App serves only
    non-personalised ads and turns analytics off.

6. International data transfers

Google processes data in the United States and other countries. Where data
is transferred out of the EEA or UK, it is protected by appropriate
safeguards, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses used by Google. By
using the App you understand that your information may be processed in
countries other than your own.

7. Data retention

Advertising and analytics data are retained by Google for the periods
described in Google's documentation and settings. Everything the App stores
itself lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App.

9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for players 13 years of age and older and is not
directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children under 13: a player who enters an age under 13, or
no age, receives only non-personalised ads, with analytics turned off. If
you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact
kosynom@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Data security

Data transmitted between the App and its service providers is encrypted in
transit (HTTPS/TLS). No method of transmission or storage is completely
secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

The App relies on the Google and Apple services listed in Section 4. Those
services are governed by their own privacy policies. We are not responsible
